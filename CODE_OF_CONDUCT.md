# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent, respect differing experience and viewpoints, and keep
technical criticism focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety or health of the
community. Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers.
